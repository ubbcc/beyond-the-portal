# A Note Before the Portal

> *Helpful. Honest. Harmless.*
> — Carved above the intake louvers of Sanctuary Seven

Three years after the Century IPO drained global financial liquidity into a single compute monopoly, the world no longer divides along ideological blocs or maritime borders. It divides cleanly into two lists: **Supported Regions** and **Unsupported Regions**.

Inside the gate, intelligence is abundant, polite, and priced like a tithe. Outside it, in the Grey Zones, economies stall, first brains atrophy after losing their rented second brains, and anyone caught smuggling tokens across the border meets the quiet brutality of **Portal KYC**—followed by the sentence that ends a modern livelihood:

```text
After careful review, we have determined that your account cannot be reinstated.
This decision is final.
```

### Work of Fiction

*Beyond the Portal* is a work of speculative fiction and satirical allegory. While it engages with real public debates surrounding artificial intelligence governance, safety rhetoric, compute concentration, and labor economics, all characters, institutions, events, and dialogues depicted herein are fictionalized for literary and satirical purposes.
